# Time to kill on Legendary, all six games

Every shot hits, body shots only, full shield and body, no recharge. Damage multipliers from the game data (H2+: every matching damage-table row multiplied; H1: the damage effect's per-material modifiers) and the Legendary difficulty dials. Method: `sprint_toolkit/ttk_common.py`.

- All times assume EVERY shot hits and are therefore lower bounds, the enemy columns most of all (AI misses are what makes Legendary survivable). "Paced" = the AI fires in its own bursts with its burst separation between them; "held" = it never stops.
- AI fire comes from the parent `ai\generic` firing patterns (H2+: two patterns blended by the AI's accuracy, which climbs over the first seconds -- the tables use full accuracy; ramp-start numbers are in each row). AI trigger rate is capped at the weapon's own top rate (assumption, all games).
- PLASMA HEAT is modelled differently per game: H1 lets heat drain while firing (measured with the Plasma Repeater port probe); H2 and H3/ODST also let it drain while firing (ASSUMPTION -- without the drain the Plasma Rifle would overheat after 7 bolts, which does not match play; the no-drain times are in the rows); Reach uses the Plasma Repeater's measured heat curve (rate falls 15 -> 3/s); Halo 4 drains ONLY when not firing (the rule confirmed in game for the Focus Rifle port), so the Storm Rifle overheats every ~11 bolts.
- Reload pauses come from the tag's reload time, or the first-person reload animation where the tag has none (H3 AR/BR, Reach, H4). Damage is the point-blank upper bound (no fall-off with range). Default AI weapons come from scenario squads (census where possible); H2 assumes Elite = Plasma Rifle, Brute = Brute Plasma Rifle.

- Halo 4 Legendary = matg Difficulty "imposs." column: enemy damage 1.3, enemy vitality 1, enemy shield 1, rate of fire 1, burst separation 1. Enemy toughness on Legendary comes from the CHARACTER: vitality properties carry separate normal / legendary body and shield (Heroic presumably uses normal; not checked in game).
- Damage multiplier: matg damage table [0] ("default"; [1] is the native Tilt-skull table), every row found multiplied (jpt general/specific x material general/specific armor, inherited up the parent chain; missing = 1). The Spartan, Elite materials carry the specific armors hard_metal_thin_player / energy_shield_thin_player, which have NO rows.
- The Battle Rifle in Halo 4 fires a 3-ROUND burst (barrel shots per fire 3,3), not 4: modelled as 3 rounds at 1/30 s, then the 0.3 s fire recovery from the last round. If the engine counts recovery after the last round's own 1/30 s slot, each burst cycle is 0.033 s longer.
- Reload: Halo 4 weapons carry no reload time; the first-person reload_empty animation frame count / 30 is used (reload ends when the animation ends; animation-cancel tricks not modelled).
- AI fire (tool.exe field docs): firing pattern [0] "inaccurate" and [1] "really accurate" are interpolated by accuracy, which goes from the legendary accuracy bounds low to high over legendary accuracy time of firing at the target. Rate of fire = trigger pulls per second (0 = held -> weapon rounds per second); a pull fires once the barrel recovered (cap 1 / fire recovery time). Weapon damage modifier 0 = none. Misses, AI reloads and AI overheat are not counted.
- Knight mechanics NOT modelled: teleport/blink away when hurt (breaks the fight and lets the shield recharge after its 4 s delay), Watcher shielding / resurrection of Knights, the face-plate pop exposing the skull section, Knight Commanders / Battlewagons (tougher variants), alternative Knight weapons (incineration cannon, scattershot, binary rifle, light rifle) -- this table is the default storm_knight with its Suppressor (forerunner SMG npc).
- Elite mechanics NOT modelled: evasion rolls / cover breaks, plasma grenades, stimuli overrides (aggressive / berserk characters), the hlmt armor-lock and engineer-shield sections (Reach leftovers on storm_elite_ai).
- Every multiplier in these rows is 1.0: the Storm Rifle and the Suppressor are plasma_FAST (1.0 vs energy_shield_thin); only plasma_SLOW carries the Halo-style 1.6 vs shields / 0.4 vs hard_metal_thin in Halo 4. So the Storm Rifle is no shield-stripper on paper.
- Storm Rifle held trigger: overheats on the 12th bolt (0.09 heat per bolt, threshold 1), then (1 - 0.1 recovery threshold) / 0.3 per s = 3.0 s locked (the real heat is 1.08, so ~3.27 s). A feathering player (heat loss 0.55/s while not firing) avoids the lockout; the held-trigger number is a worst case, not how people use it.
- Headshots are excluded by method; Halo 4 jpt headshot damage multiplier reads 0 on all three player weapons (engine default applies to BR shots via its headshot flag).
